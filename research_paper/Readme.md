## Target venue: IJCNN 2027

The paper is submitted to **IJCNN 2027**, the International Joint Conference on Neural Networks of the IEEE
Computational Intelligence Society and the International Neural Network Society (INNS), Cape Town, South Africa,
June 14 to 18, 2027. Call for papers: https://ijcnn.org/2027/authors/call-for-papers

| Date (from the call for papers) | |
|---|---|
| December 1, 2026 | Paper submissions open (the conference FAQ still says "TBD": check on the day) |
| January 31, 2027 | Regular paper deadline (time zone not stated yet) |
| March 15, 2027 | Acceptance notification |
| April 12, 2027 | Camera ready |
| June 14 to 18, 2027 | Conference |

**Format:** complete papers of no more than six (6) pages in the IEEE two-column conference proceedings format.

**Template:** the IEEE conference template, IEEEtran in conference mode (`\documentclass[conference]{IEEEtran}`),
from https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates.html. `Latex/Paper_Temp.tex` still starts with
`\documentclass[journal]{IEEEtran}` and moves to conference mode with the paper work.

**Course milestone:** the submission confirmation e-mail is due on December 8, 2026 (Final Presentation + Journal
Submission), so the paper is submitted in the first week of the window, December 1 to 8.
